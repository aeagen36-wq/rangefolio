## Install on iPhone

1. Open the link in **Safari** (not Chrome — iPhone only installs from Safari).
2. Enter your invite code.
3. Tap the Share button (square with the arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open it from the home-screen icon from now on. It works with no signal.

## Install on Android (incl. Galaxy Fold)

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Enter your invite code.
3. Tap **Install** on the banner that appears, or the ⋮ menu → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.
4. Open it from the app icon from now on. Works with no signal.

## Build 4 - what's new to try

- Tap **Target** on the Log tab: the ⌖ crosshair is your point of aim (tap
  the ⌖ button, then the target, to move it). Every tap is now saved as a
  spot, not just a zone. USPSA / IDPA / B-8 / plate targets are switchable.
- **📷 score a photo**: line the real target up in the outline, snap, tap the
  holes (hold one for a double). The hits drop into the Log page; the photo
  stays with the run.
- **Review** tab (was Coach): after a few runs with taps it draws your group
  and says what the pattern is consistent with. Set handedness under ⚙ →
  Profile so it reads the right way.
- ⚙ **Settings**: profile, coach mode (adds a Coach tab with a class builder
  and templates), roadmap, backup.
- Buttons that look greyed and dashed aren't broken - tap them and a card
  says what they'll do once accounts exist. Tap the ones you'd want.
- The app updates itself: when a new build is out it reloads once and shows
  "Updated to build N". If it seems stale, close it fully and reopen.

## Things to know

- Everything you log stays on your phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  Clearing the browser's site data wipes your book — use Book → Backup
  (JSON) if you want to keep it.
- The weather strip on the Ballistics tab is the only thing that uses the
  internet.
- Hold LOG IT to record a run. A tap on purpose does nothing — that's
  intentional.
- Fold users: it's laid out as a phone. Unfolded, it runs as a centered
  phone-width column. Tell Drew if that's annoying and we'll use the width.
